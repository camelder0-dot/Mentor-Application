Mentor with Olympus Robotics

We build a competition robot every year with high school students. We need adults who can teach what they know: engineering, machining, code, design, business, or just how to run a project.

You don't need robotics experience. Some of our best mentors had never seen an FRC robot before their first meeting. What matters is that you show up, teach patiently, and let the students do the building.

Every mentor completes the school district's background check and agrees to follow our youth protection standards before working with students. This form takes about 10 minutes.
